# Planet Vora: Singing Rock Ecosystem

## Overview

In a long distant Star system, Planet Vora is home to special rocks called **Singing Rocks**. These rocks produce a pleasant musical sound when they are healthy.

The sound from Singing Rocks makes the local inhabitants, known as **Vorans**, feel happier. Singing Rocks are important because they are connected to the happiness and daily life of the Voran population.

## Singing Rocks and Happiness

The healthier Singing Rocks that exist on Planet Vora, the happier the Vorans become.

When Vorans are happier, they spend more time and effort growing new Singing Rocks.

Growing more Singing Rocks increases the number of healthy Singing Rocks on the planet, which makes the Vorans even more happier.

## Ageing of Singing Rocks

Singing Rocks do not stay healthy forever.

Over time, every Singing Rock slowly loses its ability to produce sound and becomes a **Silent Rock**.

Silent Rocks do not make the Vorans happy.

As the number of Silent Rocks increases, Voran happiness decreases. When Vorans become less happy, they spend less time growing new Singing Rocks.


## Reviving Silent Rocks

Vorans can restore Silent Rocks using a special material called **Spark Dust**.

Spark Dust is not easy to produce. Creating Spark Dust requires time and effort from the Vorans.

When more Vorans spend time producing Spark Dust, fewer Vorans are available to grow new Singing Rocks.

After Spark Dust is produced, it can be used to restore Silent Rocks back into healthy Singing Rocks.


## Sustainability Challenge

The Vorans want to maintain a balance between growing new Singing Rocks and restoring old ones.

If too many Singing Rocks become silent, the happiness of the population decreases.

If too much effort is spent on restoration, fewer new Singing Rocks are created.
